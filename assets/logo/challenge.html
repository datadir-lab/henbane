Why the wait?

Automated crawlers now pull pages from small sites thousands of times an hour to feed model training. They ignore robots.txt and run up the hosting bill. henbane answers by asking every visitor for a few seconds of arithmetic before the page opens.

You pay that cost once. When the puzzle clears, this browser is remembered for the session, with no login and nothing tracked. A crawler reading a million pages pays it a million times, and a few seconds turns into days of compute. Cheap for a person, expensive for a machine.
